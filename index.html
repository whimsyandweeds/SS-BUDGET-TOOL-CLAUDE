Looking for grants and waivers…

Also worth planning for

  • Ask about an address confidentiality program in your state, so your new address stays private.
  • Put utilities in a different name or ask about safety holds on your account.
  • Gather ID, birth certificates, benefit and medical papers. Photograph or store copies somewhere safe.
  • Look into lease-break rights for survivors and emergency housing transfers if you are in assisted housing.
  • Ask about crime victim compensation, which can cover relocation costs.
  • Plan a new phone plan, bank account, and mail forwarding that the other person cannot see.
  • Ask legal aid about protective orders and custody, they are often free for survivors.
  • Check SNAP, Medicaid and childcare assistance, many have fast-track rules for survivors.
  • Pets: ask about safe-haven foster programs and renter pet deposits.
If you are in danger, call 911. National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 or text START to 88788. Confidential, 24/7.
